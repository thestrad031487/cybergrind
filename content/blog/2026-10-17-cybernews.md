## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've been keeping an eye on the latest developments that could impact our industry's security posture. Two stories that caught my attention are the BeyondTrust patch and the PEERSTITCH malware deployment via FortiOS flaw.

The BeyondTrust patch is a must-do for any organization using their software, as it addresses a zero-day vulnerability that has been exploited in attacks since August. This highlights the importance of timely patching and how a single vulnerability can be exploited by attackers. It's essential to prioritize patching beyondTrust and ensure it's applied promptly to prevent potential breaches.

The PEERSTITCH malware deployment via FortiOS flaw is another concerning development. The fact that this malware was able to bypass checks on tagged VPN sessions is a significant security risk, as it could allow attackers to access sensitive data without being detected. As a practitioner, I'd advise organizations using FortiOS to take immediate action and apply the necessary patches to prevent similar attacks.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** BeyondTrust should be patched immediately due to its zero-day vulnerability that has been exploited in real-world attacks since August.
