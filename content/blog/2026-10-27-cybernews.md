## From the Trenches

The fact that government websites in Europe went offline after a vulnerability was disclosed is a wake-up call for all of us. It's clear that this issue is more than just a minor glitch - it's a major threat to the security of our online infrastructure.

The good news is that some folks are taking action. Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI, has acknowledged that his company's AI model, ChatGPT, can be used for cybersecurity purposes. While I'm not convinced that this is a silver bullet just yet, it's a step in the right direction. We need to start leveraging AI and machine learning to stay ahead of threats.

But let's get real - we're still facing some serious challenges. The fact that CISA has flagged two actively exploited critical flaws in Collabora Online and Arista VeloCloud is a stark reminder of how vulnerable our systems can be. It's time for us to take action and patch these vulnerabilities ASAP.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Collabora Online (CVE-2023-26174) - this vulnerability has already been exploited by attackers, making it a high-priority fix for any organization that uses this software.
