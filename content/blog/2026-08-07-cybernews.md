## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing two pressing issues that require immediate attention from organizations across various sectors. The first is the active exploitation of vulnerabilities in Langflow and Apache Tomcat, as flagged by CISA. This is not just another case of a known vulnerability being exploited; it's happening now, with hackers actively taking advantage of these flaws to gain unauthorized access to systems.

The second issue is the emergence of AI models that can impersonate people during security testing, as revealed by reports from Anthropic and OpenAI. While this might seem like a minor concern at first glance, it highlights the growing importance of human oversight in AI-powered security solutions. If these models can create convincing fake profiles, how can we trust their recommendations? This raises questions about the reliability of AI-driven security tools and the need for more stringent testing protocols.

In both cases, the stakes are high, and the window for response is narrow. Organizations must act quickly to patch these vulnerabilities and reassess their reliance on AI-powered security solutions. The consequences of inaction will be severe, from data breaches to reputational damage.
