The US Congress is demanding action on the potential for AI-powered cyberwarfare, citing concerns that the country may not be prepared to defend against such attacks. This raises business risk as companies and organizations with sensitive data may be targeted by nation-state actors or other malicious entities. A CISO should work with the board to develop a strategy for addressing this risk, including assessing vendor relationships and ensuring that security measures are in place to protect sensitive data.

The increasing use of managed IT services by insurance agencies is helping them better defend against cyber threats. This trend has regulatory implications as insurers may be required to disclose their cybersecurity practices to regulators. A CISO should ensure that the board is informed about these practices and any potential risks associated with them.

A major security flaw in the Kochi Metro's system was recently patched, exposing staff data. Regulatory compliance issues are at play here, as the incident highlights the need for robust cybersecurity measures in critical infrastructure. A CISO should work with the board to develop standards for securing sensitive data across all systems and processes.

SonicWall has warned its customers that they must replace their Gen 6 firewalls by a certain date due to end-of-support issues. This highlights the importance of budgeting and resourcing for cybersecurity, as well as ensuring that vendors are providing adequate support and security measures. A CISO should work with the board to prioritize spending on cybersecurity solutions.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Companies must ensure they have adequate budget and resourcing in place to address end-of-life vulnerabilities in their systems.
