## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a clear trend emerging from recent news stories. The repeated mention of Visa's expansion of support for its clients and the industry is a stark reminder that organizations are navigating uncharted territory when it comes to AI-powered cyber threats.

This is not just a matter of semantics; it highlights the growing recognition that AI-enabled attacks require specialized defenses. Tech companies, including those in the payment processing space like Visa, are acknowledging this shift and are proactively adapting their security measures. This is a positive development, as it suggests that organizations are taking proactive steps to bolster their defenses against increasingly sophisticated threats.

However, it's also clear that some organizations still need guidance on how to effectively respond to these emerging threats. This is where the work of companies like PaperCut comes in – releasing emergency patches for exploited flaws shows that even well-established players are not immune to the risks posed by AI-powered attacks.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** PaperCut's second emergency patch for conductai (Kitploit.com) matters because it highlights the urgent need for organizations to stay on top of their software updates and patch cycles in order to prevent exploitation by attackers.
