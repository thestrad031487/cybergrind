## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've been following the latest news on supermarket cyber attacks, and it's clear that third-party systems are leaving these businesses vulnerable to exploitation. According to ABC News (AU), supermarkets are being targeted by hackers who can gain access to their systems through weak connections with external vendors. This is a stark reminder of the importance of due diligence when it comes to third-party relationships.

The situation has been exacerbated by the use of AI-powered chatbots, as highlighted in Venturesquare.net. The article reveals that even these supposedly secure tools can be used to check for sensitive information, raising concerns about their potential misuse. As cybersecurity professionals, we need to be aware of the risks associated with these emerging technologies and ensure they are properly secured.

Meanwhile, the French supermarket hack is a stark reminder of the devastating consequences of a successful cyber attack. With reports of fires and food spoilage at multiple locations, it's clear that this incident has had significant real-world impacts. As practitioners, we need to be prepared for such events and have effective strategies in place to mitigate their effects.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** The French supermarket fridges affected by the hack are vulnerable to a known issue with the Samsung SmartThings IoT platform, which has been patched multiple times already.
