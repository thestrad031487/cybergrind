## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm constantly on high alert for potential threats that could compromise our digital infrastructure. Two recent stories caught my attention because they highlight critical vulnerabilities and the need for immediate action.

The first story that stands out is the one about hackers exploiting N-central flaws to infect 21 servers, including 19 running Hotfix 2. This is a stark reminder of how easy it is for attackers to find weaknesses in our systems and exploit them. As security professionals, we often focus on patching vulnerabilities quickly, but this story shows that even with the latest hotfixes, we can still be caught off guard.

The second story that resonates with me is the one about PHP2Shell exposing cybersecurity's new AI paradox: Finding flaws is becoming easier than fixing them. This is a wake-up call for us to rethink our approach to vulnerability management. With AI-powered tools becoming more prevalent in our arsenal, we need to ensure that our own processes are keeping pace. We can't rely solely on automation; human expertise and vigilance are still essential in identifying and mitigating threats.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Apache HTTP Server patches critical HTTP/2 remote code execution flaw - because it's a fundamental web server that underpins many online services, this vulnerability is a high-priority fix for any organization relying on the internet.
