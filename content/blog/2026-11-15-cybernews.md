## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing some concerning trends that demand attention from organizations of all sizes. The recent incident involving Check Point gateways hacked via PHP2Shell to deploy VMware ESXi wiper is a stark reminder of how quickly vulnerabilities can be exploited. This is not just a matter of patching individual systems; it's about understanding the attack surface of your entire infrastructure.

The same vulnerability was also used in another exploit, this time to infect 21 servers, including 19 running Hotfix 2. The fact that hackers were able to take advantage of these known vulnerabilities is alarming, and it highlights the importance of keeping up-to-date with security patches and monitoring for suspicious activity. Organizations can't afford to wait until an attack occurs; they need to be proactive in identifying and remediating vulnerabilities before they become a problem.

The PHP2Shell vulnerability is particularly concerning because it was designed to be used by attackers, making it easier for them to gain access to systems. This underscores the importance of having robust security controls in place, including intrusion detection and prevention systems, as well as regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Check Point gateways need to be patched ASAP to prevent further exploitation of PHP2Shell vulnerabilities.
