The recent URL encoding bypass in Cisco SD-WAN's software poses a significant risk to federal agencies, as it grants unauthenticated admin access. This vulnerability allows attackers to potentially gain control of the entire network, highlighting the importance of patching and updating software promptly.

In addition to the immediate need for patching, this incident also underscores the value of implementing robust monitoring and incident response plans. The CISO should work closely with the board to ensure that such plans are in place and regularly tested, including conducting tabletop exercises and providing quarterly reports on the effectiveness of these efforts.

Furthermore, this vulnerability highlights the importance of vendor risk management. The CISO should conduct a thorough assessment of Cisco's security posture, including evaluating their incident response procedures, patching policies, and software updates. This will help ensure that similar vulnerabilities are not exploited in the future.

The cost of this incident could be substantial, with potential losses ranging from millions to billions of dollars. As such, the CISO should work closely with the board to develop a contingency plan for addressing any potential financial losses or reputational damage.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The organization should prioritize patching and updating software promptly, implementing robust monitoring and incident response plans, and conducting regular vendor risk assessments.
