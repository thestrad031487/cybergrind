## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing more and more evidence that AI is becoming a significant threat to corporate security. The question is, what's driving this trend? According to Digital Journal, insider threats are evolving as AI exposes hidden pathways across corporate systems. This means that even trusted employees can inadvertently or intentionally compromise the security of an organization using AI-powered tools.

Another worrying development is the emergence of AI-driven network breaches. DIVD says Zammad zero-days enabled these attacks, highlighting the need for better patch management and vulnerability disclosure practices. It's clear that attackers are exploiting known vulnerabilities to deploy web shells and harvest authentication secrets - a classic tactic with a modern twist.

In light of these threats, it's essential to prioritize secure software updates and patching. As I'm reviewing the latest security advisories, one thing stands out: attackers have been exploiting CVE-2026-88772 in NetScaler since early September. **🔧 Patch Priority:** NetScaler must be patched immediately to prevent further exploitation of this zero-day vulnerability.
