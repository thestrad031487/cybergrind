The growing trend of companies leaving critical vulnerabilities unpatched has significant business risk implications. 

This oversight can lead to costly lawsuits, reputational damage, and loss of customer trust, which can ultimately result in a substantial financial hit for the organization.

A CISO should take proactive steps to address this issue by implementing robust patch management processes, providing regular security audits and vulnerability assessments, and conducting thorough risk assessments to identify potential areas of exposure. 

Moreover, considering regulatory implications, companies may need to ensure compliance with industry standards such as NIST Cybersecurity Framework or PCI-DSS, which often require vulnerability patches and regular security monitoring.

Given the increasing sophistication of cyberattacks, it is also essential for a CISO to engage with board members in risk discussions and prepare a robust incident response plan that outlines procedures for identifying and responding to potential breaches. 

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Companies should prioritize proactive patch management and regular security assessments to mitigate business risks associated with critical vulnerabilities.
