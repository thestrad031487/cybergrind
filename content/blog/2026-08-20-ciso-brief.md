The growing threat of AI-driven cyberattacks is a serious concern for businesses. An executive from OpenAI recently stated that the solution to AI being used for malicious purposes may be more AI itself, highlighting the need for robust cybersecurity measures.

This underscores the importance of investing in AI-powered security tools and monitoring systems to detect and respond to potential threats. A CISO should also consider conducting regular risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities in their organization's AI systems.

Additionally, as AI continues to permeate various industries, organizations must ensure compliance with relevant regulations, such as GDPR and CCPA, which address data protection and security standards for AI-driven systems.

Furthermore, the use of AI-powered tools can also impact a company's insurance coverage. Some insurers may not cover losses resulting from AI-driven attacks, so it's essential to review insurance policies and consider purchasing additional coverage.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations should prioritize investing in robust AI-powered security measures to mitigate the growing threat of AI-driven cyberattacks.
