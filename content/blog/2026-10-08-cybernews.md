## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing two trends that are making my job more complicated by the day. The first is the rise of AI-designed viruses and malware that can evade traditional security measures. According to "How to Defend Against AI-Designed Viruses" (War on the Rocks), these threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated, making it harder for even the most advanced systems to detect and respond.

The second trend I'm noticing is the growing use of agentic security, where AI systems are being used to fight cyber threats on their own. This is exactly what happens when AI starts fighting cyber threats on its own, as described in "What happens when AI starts fighting cyber threats on its own?" (The Times of India). While this sounds like a promising development, I'm concerned about the potential risks and unintended consequences of relying on autonomous systems to defend against threats.

As we move forward, it's clear that we need to develop more effective strategies for managing vulnerability risks in the age of AI. This is exactly what CISO perspectives on managing vulnerability risks in the age of AI (Microsoft.com) are addressing, but I believe we need to take a closer look at the specific technologies and systems being used.
