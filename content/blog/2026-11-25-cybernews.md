## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've been seeing a lot of buzz around critical vulnerabilities that are being exploited by attackers. One of the most concerning stories is the Critical Java Vulnerability that's raising new security concerns for banks (pymnts.com). This vulnerability is serious and can be used to gain unauthorized access to systems, which could lead to significant financial losses.

Another story that caught my attention is the SAP NetWeaver and Cisco Expressway flaws being exploited in attacks (Securityweek.com). These vulnerabilities highlight the importance of keeping software up-to-date and patching quickly. As a practitioner, I've seen firsthand how quickly attackers can move laterally through a network once they gain initial access.

It's clear that banks and organizations need to take these threats seriously and prioritize patching vulnerable systems ASAP. With the accelerating nature of cyber disruptions, it's essential to stay vigilant and proactive in our security measures.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** SAP NetWeaver due to its widespread use in enterprise environments and the potential for significant lateral movement if exploited by attackers.
