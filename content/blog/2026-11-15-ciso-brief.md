The recent grounding of flights, crashing of boats, and tech meltdown have experts sounding the alarm about a potential "mothership" theory behind these events. As a CISO, it's essential to consider the business implications of such threats, including potential disruption to supply chains and operations.

From a regulatory perspective, this incident highlights the need for enhanced threat intelligence sharing among nations and the importance of cooperation in combating transnational cybercrime. The US government should prioritize developing and implementing effective countermeasures against these types of attacks.

In terms of risk management, CISOs should conduct thorough vulnerability assessments to identify potential weaknesses in their organization's systems and infrastructure. This includes reviewing third-party vendors' security practices and ensuring that all necessary updates and patches are installed.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The board should request a comprehensive risk assessment report to determine the likelihood and potential impact of similar attacks on the company's operations.
