The increasing threat to Europe's critical infrastructure is a serious concern for businesses operating in the region, as highlighted in the latest Enisa EU Cyber Threats Report. 

This report confirms what many have feared – cyber attacks are becoming a significant risk to Europe's technology backbone, and organizations must take this seriously. The board should be aware of the business risks associated with disruptions to critical infrastructure, such as supply chain breaks or loss of customer trust.

To mitigate these risks, CISOs should engage in active dialogue with their boards about developing incident response plans that prioritize data protection and business continuity. 

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations operating in Europe must prioritize developing incident response plans that address the increasing threat to critical infrastructure.
