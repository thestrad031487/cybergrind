## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a shift in the way organizations approach security, particularly with the increasing presence of AI agents in the workforce. Proofpoint Protect's recent insights highlight the importance of intent-based security, where the focus is on understanding the intentions behind an actor's actions rather than just their actions themselves. This approach can help reduce false positives and improve incident response times.

However, this trend also raises concerns about accountability and transparency in AI-driven systems. As these agents become more prevalent, it's essential that we establish clear guidelines and regulations for their use. The EU's warning about the potential arrival of Q-Day before quantum computers are commercially viable only adds to the urgency. Quantum computing has the potential to exponentially increase computational power, making current security measures obsolete.

The Trump administration's release of a blueprint to protect election infrastructure ahead of the midterms is also noteworthy. This move acknowledges the growing threat landscape and demonstrates a commitment to securing critical infrastructure. As practitioners, we need to be aware of these developments and adjust our strategies accordingly.
