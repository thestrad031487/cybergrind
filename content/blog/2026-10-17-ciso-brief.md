BeyondTrust patches zero-day exploited in attacks since August. This highlights the need for a robust patch management process and frequent vulnerability assessments to prevent similar incidents.

Regulatory implications arise from the fact that many organizations rely on third-party vendors like BeyondTrust for their security needs, making it essential for CISOs to conduct thorough risk assessments of these vendors and ensure they are addressing any identified vulnerabilities promptly.

Given this scenario, a CISO should prioritize enhancing their vendor risk management practices, including regular vulnerability testing, contract reviews, and clear communication with vendors about incident response and remediation plans. Furthermore, conducting a thorough review of internal policies and procedures to ensure alignment with industry standards is crucial.

A robust Incident Response Plan that includes external threat intelligence feeds and regular tabletop exercises should be in place to minimize the impact of similar incidents in the future.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Cybersecurity teams must prioritize vendor risk management and incident response planning to mitigate the risk of zero-day exploits.
