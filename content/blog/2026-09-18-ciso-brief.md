Cisco's recent warnings about zero-day exploits in their Identity Service Engine (ISE) highlight the ongoing threat landscape, where a single vulnerability can be exploited to compromise large-scale networks. This vulnerability, combined with the growing sophistication of cyber attackers, underscores the need for robust threat intelligence and incident response planning. To mitigate this risk, I recommend that our organization conduct a thorough review of our current threat intelligence processes and ensure that they are aligned with industry best practices.

The increasing use of AI-powered tools by threat actors also poses significant risks to our organization's security posture. As Hackuity raises $19M to help security teams manage AI-found vulnerabilities, it is essential that we stay ahead of the curve in terms of vulnerability management and remediation strategies. I suggest that we engage with Hackuity to explore potential partnership opportunities and develop a comprehensive plan for managing AI-related vulnerabilities.

The rise of ransomware attacks, including those targeting Russian enterprises, highlights the need for robust backup and disaster recovery systems. Our organization should review its current backup and DR procedures to ensure they are compliant with industry standards and can recover data in the event of an attack.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize threat intelligence, vulnerability management, and backup and disaster recovery planning to stay ahead of emerging cyber threats.
