Hackers can target your wealth adviser’s WhatsApp, and it's a serious business risk. Wealth management firms are increasingly using WhatsApp for client communication, but this also means they're vulnerable to phishing attacks or malware-laced messages that could compromise sensitive information. A CISO should ensure that all staff use secure messaging apps like Signal or Telegram instead of unsecured platforms like WhatsApp.

The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) has issued warnings about the risks of using unsecured messaging apps for client communication, and regulators are likely to crack down on firms that don't take adequate security measures. A CISO should work with finance leaders to develop policies around secure messaging and ensure compliance with FINRA guidelines.

If a wealth management firm is breached through a phishing attack or malware infection, it could lead to significant reputational damage and financial losses. A CISO should review the firm's incident response plan to ensure that it includes procedures for responding to security incidents involving client data.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Failure to adopt secure messaging practices and comply with FINRA guidelines will result in regulatory action and potential fines.
