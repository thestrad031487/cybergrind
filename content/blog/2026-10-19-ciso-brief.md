Using an AI Agent Makes You a Sucker for Phishing Emails is a reminder that attackers are increasingly using AI-powered tools to craft highly realistic phishing emails. This highlights the need for robust security awareness training and regular phishing simulation exercises to ensure employees can spot these advanced threats.

The exploitation of CVE-2026-88771 in NetScaler, a widely used web application firewall, underscores the importance of keeping software up-to-date and patching vulnerabilities promptly. As a result, CISOs should prioritize regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify potential entry points for attackers.

A recent zero-day exploit in BeyondTrust highlights the need for robust endpoint security measures. Given the severity of this attack, CISOs should review their endpoint protection policies to ensure they cover even the most sophisticated threats.

Google's warnings about suspected China-linked hackers exploiting the BeyondTrust flaw serve as a reminder of the global threat landscape and the importance of maintaining robust security posture. As a result, CISOs should assess the overall risk profile of their organization and consider implementing additional security controls.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Effective cybersecurity requires not only technical expertise but also awareness of broader business risks and regulatory implications.
