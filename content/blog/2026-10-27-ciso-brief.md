The European government websites' recent downtime due to a PHP vulnerability is a stark reminder of the risks posed by unpatched software. This incident highlights the need for robust testing and validation procedures in-house, as relying solely on external vendors can leave organizations vulnerable to exploitation.

A similar risk exists with AI-powered cybersecurity solutions, such as ChatGPT, which are increasingly being adopted without adequate scrutiny. As AI-powered systems become more prevalent, it's essential for boards to establish clear guidelines and oversight mechanisms to ensure that these tools are used responsibly and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

The recent discovery of a critical flaw in Collabora Online and Arista VeloCloud by CISA underscores the importance of staying up-to-date with the latest security patches. This incident serves as a warning to organizations to prioritize vulnerability management and ensure that their systems are patched regularly, lest they become vulnerable to exploitation.

In light of these incidents, it's crucial for boards to reassess their cybersecurity posture and allocate sufficient resources to address potential risks. This may involve increasing budget allocations for cybersecurity initiatives or investing in tools that can help detect and respond to threats more effectively.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize vulnerability management and invest in robust testing procedures to mitigate the risk of exploitation by unpatched software.
