The rise of AI-driven insider threats poses a significant risk to corporate security, as attackers can now exploit vulnerabilities in systems and use AI-powered tools to move undetected across networks. This threat requires boards to consider the potential consequences of inadequate security measures, including data breaches and reputational damage. A CISO should prioritize developing policies and procedures to detect and prevent AI-driven insider threats.

Furthermore, the exploitation of Zimbra's vulnerability by attackers highlights the need for organizations to stay up-to-date with patch management and ensure that third-party software is secure. Boards should demand regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities in vendor-supplied software.

The growing use of artificial intelligence also raises concerns about data discovery and disclosure, as AI-powered tools can uncover sensitive information without human intervention. A CISO should ensure that the organization has a robust vulnerability disclosure policy in place, including clear guidelines for reporting and remediating discovered flaws.

In light of these developments, it is essential to consider investing in advanced threat detection capabilities to stay ahead of emerging security threats.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Boards should prioritize developing policies and procedures to detect and prevent AI-driven insider threats.
