Trump's support for unregulated AI poses a significant business risk, as it may lead to compromised US national security. If implemented without proper controls, this technology could inadvertently create vulnerabilities that adversaries exploit. The CISO should recommend policy updates and regulatory engagement with government agencies to establish clear guidelines for AI development.

Regulatory implications arise from China's pursuit of unregulated AI, which may be seen as a response to the US strategy. This development highlights the need for US organizations to stay informed about international AI developments and potential implications. The CISO should monitor Chinese regulations and engage in diplomatic efforts to ensure that any US responses or counter-strategies align with domestic laws.

The ShinyHunters hackers' attacks on Oracle's PeopleSoft underscore the importance of robust security posture, including incident response planning. As a result, the CISO should prioritize vendor risk assessments, ensuring that third-party providers adhere to secure standards and implement effective breach notification procedures.

CISA's warning about Sharepoint, WSO2, and Adobe Commerce flaws demonstrates the need for ongoing patch management and vulnerability scanning. The CISO should emphasize regular security updates and training for employees on software application security best practices to mitigate potential risks.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize AI governance and regulatory compliance to ensure that emerging technologies align with national interests and protect against cyber threats.
