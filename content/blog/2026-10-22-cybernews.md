## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a disturbing trend where smart devices are becoming increasingly vulnerable to attacks. A recent article highlights how your car's advanced features are being exploited by hackers, with New Atlas revealing that these "smartest" features are actually opening doors for malicious actors. This is a wake-up call for all of us who rely on connected devices in our daily lives.

On the other hand, I'm encouraged to see efforts from organizations like CISA and Tenable expanding their cybersecurity offerings. The recent order from CISA to patch exploited Plex2Shell, Siemens, and SiteGuard flaws is a clear indication that these vulnerabilities are being taken seriously, and it's essential for federal agencies to take immediate action. Meanwhile, Tenable's expansion of its Open Security Ecosystem aims to accelerate AI security outcomes, which is a promising development in the fight against emerging threats.

As we move forward, it's crucial to prioritize patching and vulnerability management. **🔧 Patch Priority:** Cisco's ASA series firewall must be patched for the Siemens vulnerability ASAP, as this could leave organizations exposed to lateral movement attacks.
