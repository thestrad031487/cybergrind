The recent surge in zero-day exploits and vulnerabilities highlights the need for robust security controls to protect an organization's assets. 

Microsoft's announcement of patching 974 flaws, including two Windows zero-days, underscores the importance of timely and effective vulnerability management. This should prompt a thorough review of the incident response plan to ensure it is aligned with the organization's risk tolerance.

The N-able N-central pre-auth RCE flaw exploitation in the wild serves as a warning about the need for robust security controls at the entry point of networks, including multi-factor authentication and secure access validation protocols.

The development of AI-powered computer worms and kernel vulnerabilities such as SAP's "OVERPASS" vulnerability raises concerns about the potential risks associated with emerging technologies. A comprehensive risk assessment should be conducted to identify areas where AI could introduce new attack surfaces or exploit vulnerabilities.

India's efforts to enhance security architectures for AI systems demonstrate a growing recognition of the need for robust security measures in this realm. This highlights the importance of investing in threat intelligence and research to stay ahead of emerging threats.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The board should prioritize incident response planning and vulnerability management to mitigate the risk of zero-day exploits and ensure business continuity.
