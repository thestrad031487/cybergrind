## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've been keeping an eye on recent developments that warrant attention from both security professionals and business owners alike. Two stories that caught my attention are Check Point's vulnerabilities and the potential risks of AI-enabled penetration testing platforms.

Check Point's addition to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog is a stark reminder of the importance of staying up-to-date with patching cycles. The PHP2Shell vulnerability, in particular, highlights the need for timely patching, as hackers have already exploited it to deploy malware and gain unauthorized access to systems. This is not a drill; organizations that haven't patched these vulnerabilities yet are putting themselves at risk of being compromised.

On the other hand, the concept of AI-enabled penetration testing platforms raises interesting questions about the role of automation in security testing. While I appreciate the idea of using AI to streamline and improve testing processes, I'm cautious about the potential risks associated with relying too heavily on machine learning algorithms. As Proofpoint Researcher notes, hackers' overreliance on AI can result in weaker cyber-attacks, which is a sobering reminder that no technology is foolproof.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Check Point gateways need to be patched ASAP due to the PHP2Shell vulnerability and its potential impact on VMware ESXi systems.
