## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a rise in AI-enabled cyberattacks that's making it essential for organizations to be prepared. The recent publication of an AI researcher's preparedness checklist by Naturalnews.com is a welcome addition to this conversation. It highlights the need for organizations to develop strategies for detecting and mitigating these types of attacks, which are becoming increasingly sophisticated.

The fact that Chinese hackers have been using VMware ESX zero-day vulnerabilities to launch attacks is a stark reminder of the importance of keeping software up-to-date and patched. The use of PHP2Shell and Fragnesia chained to root firewalls is particularly concerning, as it suggests that attackers are finding ways to bypass traditional security controls.

In light of these developments, I'm seeing more organizations turning to open-weight AI models as a way to stay ahead of the threat curve. However, this also means that there's a growing patch gap that needs to be addressed - and quickly. Organizations need to prioritize their patching efforts to avoid becoming the next target for these increasingly sophisticated attacks.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** IBM z/OS requires immediate attention due to its potential vulnerability to exploitation by Chinese hackers using VMware ESX zero-day vulnerabilities.
