## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing two trends that are making my job a lot more interesting - and a lot harder. The first is the rise of AI-powered threat hunting platforms, which promise to make it easier for defenders to stay one step ahead of attackers. A new open-source platform using AI agents to hunt threats is just the latest example, and while it's exciting to see innovation in this space, I'm also seeing how quickly these tools can be used to launch devastating attacks.

The second trend that's got me scratching my head is the discovery of a critical flaw in PHP, which is used by 70% of websites. This is a classic case of "tech debt" coming due, where years of patching and updating are left undone, leaving systems vulnerable to exploitation. It's a stark reminder that stealthy attacks can be just as deadly as loud ones - and it highlights the importance of prioritizing security in every aspect of software development.

In both cases, I'm seeing the need for greater cooperation and coordination between defenders and developers. We need to work together to identify and fix vulnerabilities before they're exploited by attackers. As we move forward, I'll be keeping a close eye on these trends - and making sure that my team is prepared to respond to whatever threats come our way.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Collabora Online CVE-2023-2616, which was flagged by CISA as being actively exploited, needs immediate attention from developers and organizations using the software.
