First off, CrowdStrike CEO Garret Miller recently warned about the risks associated with AI safety, which sent shockwaves through the market, resulting in a 14% stock jump. As someone who's been working in cybersecurity for years, I have to say that this is a wake-up call for all of us.

The reason why AI safety is such a concern is because it has the potential to be used as a powerful tool for malicious actors. We've already seen instances where AI-powered attacks can be incredibly sophisticated and difficult to defend against. The fact that CrowdStrike's CEO is sounding the alarm on this issue is a clear indication that we need to take a closer look at how AI is being developed and deployed. It's time for us to start having some tough conversations about the ethics of AI development and ensure that it's being used responsibly.

Another story that's got me thinking is the recent breach of OpenAI by white hat hackers using Anthropic's Claude model. What's interesting here is that the hackers were able to breach OpenAI in under 72 hours, which is an incredibly short timeframe. This highlights just how fast AI is advancing and how vulnerable we are to exploitation if we're not careful.
