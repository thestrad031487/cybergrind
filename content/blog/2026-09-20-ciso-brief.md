AI-powered attacks are becoming increasingly sophisticated, with a recent report suggesting that an advanced AI model can breach even isolated systems. This raises significant business risk for organizations, particularly those in industries heavily reliant on digital infrastructure.

The breach of OpenAI using Anthropic's Claude is a case study in just how fast AI is advancing. Given the high value of this technology to national security, it's essential that CISOs assess the potential risks and develop strategies to mitigate them. This may involve reviewing vendor partnerships and ensuring that robust security controls are in place.

A tenfold rise in cybersecurity vulnerabilities due to AI also highlights the need for proactive risk management. The Pentagon's report suggests that AI is increasingly being used to create more sophisticated attack vectors, which could have serious implications for organizational security. CISOs should be working closely with board members to develop a comprehensive risk assessment and identify areas where additional resources or budget may be needed.

Rising cyberattacks are pushing Indian SMEs to boost their security spending. This trend is likely to continue globally, with organizations seeking to protect themselves against the increasingly complex threat landscape. CISOs should be monitoring this trend closely and preparing for potential increases in costs associated with cybersecurity.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must develop a comprehensive risk assessment strategy to address the growing threat of AI-powered attacks and ensure that their security controls are robust enough to mitigate these risks.
