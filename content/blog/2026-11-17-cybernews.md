## From the Trenches

I'm seeing a lot of low-hanging fruit out there for attackers to exploit right now. The BO Team's successful use of Log4Shell in VMware vCenter to compromise a Russian firm's code-signing server is a stark reminder that even well-known vulnerabilities can be leveraged by sophisticated actors. This highlights the importance of keeping software up-to-date and patching known vulnerabilities promptly.

The fact that a company like this was breached despite having robust security measures in place raises questions about how effective counterintelligence training can be for CEOs. Bodyguards aren't enough to protect against insider threats or sophisticated attacks, and it's clear that more needs to be done to educate executives on the importance of cybersecurity. This is especially true in today's landscape where attackers are getting increasingly creative.

The recent exploits I've seen highlight just how important it is to stay vigilant and patch known vulnerabilities quickly. VMware ESXi wiper, for example, can cause significant damage to systems if not addressed promptly. It's a good reminder that no system is completely secure, and constant monitoring and maintenance are necessary to stay ahead of attackers.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** VMware ESXi must be patched immediately to prevent potential wiper attacks.
