Cyberattacks as a Tactic of Digital Transnational Repression: Freedom House - TNR Watch (freedomhouse.org)

The use of cyberattacks by authoritarian regimes to suppress dissent and opposition is becoming increasingly common. This tactic can have significant implications for businesses operating globally, particularly those in industries with close ties to governments. As the threat landscape evolves, CISOs should prioritize monitoring for signs of state-sponsored hacking and advising leadership on how to mitigate these risks.

Regulatory compliance will also play a critical role in this scenario. Businesses must ensure they are adhering to existing regulations while also taking steps to prevent unauthorized cyber activities. This may involve collaborating with government agencies or seeking guidance from experts familiar with international cybersecurity laws.

To address the growing threat, CISOs should engage with their boards on strategies for mitigating state-sponsored hacking, including incident response planning and risk management frameworks.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Companies operating globally must prioritize monitoring for signs of state-sponsored hacking to protect against increasingly sophisticated threats.
