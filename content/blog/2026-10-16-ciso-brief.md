The exploitation of this vulnerability also highlights the need for more stringent regulatory compliance. With increasing cyber threats, organizations must ensure that they are implementing robust security protocols to protect sensitive data. This includes regular audits and penetration testing to identify vulnerabilities before an attack occurs.

Given the severity of the exploit, I recommend that CISOs immediately assess their PAN-OS infrastructure and develop a mitigation plan with the board's support. This should include assessing any existing backups or incident response plans in place to minimize potential damage from such attacks.
