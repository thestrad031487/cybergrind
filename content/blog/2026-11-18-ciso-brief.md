The rise of agentic threats has significant implications for cybersecurity posture. Google's recent warning about autonomous cyberattacks highlights the growing concern that AI can be used to launch sophisticated and targeted attacks, making it essential for security leaders to prioritize threat intelligence and incident response capabilities.

Regulatory bodies will likely take notice of this trend and may update guidelines or standards to address the use of AI in cybersecurity. CISOs should monitor these developments closely and advocate for the necessary investments in AI-powered security solutions that can detect and respond to agentic threats.

In addition, companies must also consider the potential risks associated with relying on third-party vendors that may not have robust controls in place to mitigate AI-driven threats. Board reporting on vendor risk assessments and security posture should be a priority to ensure that boards are informed about these emerging risks.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Companies should prioritize threat intelligence and incident response capabilities to address the growing concern of agentic threats in cybersecurity.
