The increasing threat of AI-powered cyberattacks requires board-level attention. The use of PHP2SHELL exploits to breach US government-affiliated firewalls and the potential for AI to compromise digital infrastructure pose significant business risks. A CISO should prioritize assessing vendor risk and ensuring robust incident response protocols in place, as well as maintaining open communication with the board on these matters.

The growing reliance on AI-driven systems also raises regulatory compliance concerns, particularly regarding data protection and privacy. Boards should ensure that their organizations are adapting to emerging regulations and guidelines, such as those related to artificial intelligence and machine learning.

In addition, the increasing sophistication of cyberattacks highlights the need for proactive budgeting decisions. CISOs should work with the board to allocate sufficient resources for cybersecurity measures, including investing in AI-powered security solutions that can detect and respond to threats more effectively.

The growing threat landscape also underscores the importance of insurance coverage for cybersecurity-related risks. Boards should review their existing insurance policies to ensure they provide adequate coverage for potential losses resulting from AI-powered cyberattacks.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize proactive risk assessment and budgeting decisions in response to emerging AI-powered cyber threats.
