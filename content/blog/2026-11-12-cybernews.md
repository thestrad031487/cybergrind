## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a familiar pattern emerging - many organizations are being exploited by known vulnerabilities that could've been patched months ago. The recent case of Cisco BroadWorks and Openwave flaws actively being exploited despite available patches is a stark reminder of how quickly attackers move once they find an entry point.

This highlights the importance of timely patching and regular vulnerability assessments. It's not just about having the latest security tools, but also about staying on top of the ever-evolving threat landscape. I've seen too many cases where organizations have been caught off guard by exploits that could've been prevented with a simple patch or update.

The fact that 45% of surveyed IT providers take months to patch vulnerabilities is particularly concerning. This not only puts their own systems at risk but also exposes their customers and partners to potential attacks. It's time for organizations to prioritize patching and vulnerability management, and to hold themselves accountable for staying up-to-date with the latest security patches.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Cisco BroadWorks due to its widespread use in business communications systems and the high likelihood of exploitation by threat actors.
