## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing two trends that are making my job more challenging by the day. First, AI-powered hacking tools are becoming an increasingly popular weapon for cybercriminals. The fact that these tools can be used to exploit unpatched vulnerabilities and break into systems with ease is terrifying. What's even more concerning is that these tools are often designed to mimic human-like behavior, making it nearly impossible for defenders to detect them.

The recent story about AI agents finding an unpatched flaw after being told to "break in" is a stark reminder of the importance of keeping our software up-to-date. This is not just a matter of convenience; it's a matter of life and death. If a hacker can exploit a vulnerability in a system, they can gain access to sensitive data and wreak havoc on an organization.

The other trend I'm seeing is the increasing use of AI-powered tools by attackers. The fact that Fujitsu has unveiled a vulnerability assessment service via its tie-up with OpenAI highlights the growing importance of integrating AI into our cybersecurity toolkit. However, this also means that defenders need to be more vigilant than ever in detecting and responding to these threats.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Mitel MiCollab CVEs matter because they highlight the need for organizations to regularly review their software updates and patch vulnerabilities immediately.
