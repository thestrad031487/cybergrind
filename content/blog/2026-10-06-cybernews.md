## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm always on the lookout for potential vulnerabilities that could be exploited by malicious actors. Two recent stories caught my attention because they highlight the importance of protecting sensitive information and maintaining the integrity of critical infrastructure.

The first story that stood out to me is about hackers targeting wealth advisers' WhatsApp accounts (The Times of India). This may seem like a trivial threat, but it's a reminder that even seemingly innocuous communication channels can be vulnerable to exploitation. Wealth advisers handle sensitive client information, and if their WhatsApp conversations are compromised, the consequences could be severe. As a practitioner, I would advise wealth advisers to consider implementing additional security measures, such as using end-to-end encryption or switching to more secure communication platforms.

The second story that resonated with me is about a researcher breaking into Microsoft's analytics service and exploiting a NetScaler RCE 0-day (Help Net Security). This highlights the importance of keeping software up to date and patching vulnerabilities in a timely manner. As a practitioner, I would stress the need for organizations to prioritize patching their systems, especially those that are critical to their operations. The fact that this vulnerability was exploited within hours of its discovery underscores the urgency of keeping software current.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Cisco SD-WAN's URL encoding bypass grant unauthenticated admin access, and federal agencies have 24 hours to address this issue.
