Acronis MDR empowers service providers with detection, response and true business resilience. This new offering from Acronis could significantly expand the reach of managed security services for organizations, especially small and medium-sized businesses that may not have the resources to invest in their own security infrastructure.

The implications of this for our organization are that we need to consider whether investing in MDR is a viable option for us, particularly if we're looking to improve our response times to security incidents. We should also assess whether our current security service providers have similar capabilities and whether they can provide the same level of business resilience as Acronis' new offering.

This development highlights the growing importance of managed security services in today's complex cybersecurity landscape. As a result, we need to ensure that our own security service providers are equipped to handle emerging threats and offer robust business resilience solutions.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** We should prioritize assessing our current security service providers' capabilities against those offered by Acronis MDR and exploring the benefits of investing in this technology for improved business resilience.
