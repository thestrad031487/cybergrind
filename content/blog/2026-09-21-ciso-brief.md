The US Department of Defense's FY 2024 budget allocates $3.2 billion for cybersecurity, up from $1.8 billion in 2019.

This represents a significant increase in funding for the US military's cybersecurity efforts, which is likely driven by concerns about national security risks. The increased investment will likely lead to improved defenses against advanced threats and enhanced capabilities for the US Department of Defense. A CISO should monitor this trend closely, as it highlights the growing importance of investing in robust cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive information.

The increased funding also underscores the need for effective risk management and mitigation strategies, particularly in light of rising cyberattacks on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). This is a business risk that organizations must address proactively, by assessing their own security posture and implementing measures to protect against emerging threats.

In terms of regulatory implications, this increased funding may also lead to greater scrutiny from lawmakers and regulators regarding the use of taxpayer dollars for cybersecurity initiatives. A CISO should be prepared to provide clear guidance on how this investment aligns with organizational goals and will mitigate potential risks.
