The increasing threat to water utilities is a critical business risk that requires immediate attention from security leadership.

Water utilities are often underfunded and understaffed, making them more vulnerable to cyber attacks. The recent Water Watch Center partnership between the Water Utilities Group and DEF CON offshoot highlights the growing concern about the potential for water systems to be compromised. This presents a significant risk to the supply chain and could have devastating consequences for public health.

To mitigate this risk, I recommend that security teams conduct a thorough assessment of their current security posture, including penetration testing and vulnerability assessments. I also suggest engaging with vendors to ensure they are taking proactive measures to secure their systems and data related to water utilities.

Furthermore, I believe it is essential to establish clear policies and procedures for incident response and communication in the event of a breach. This could include developing a comprehensive emergency response plan that outlines roles and responsibilities for security teams, executives, and board members.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The Water Utilities Group's partnership with DEF CON offshoot highlights the need for immediate attention to the growing threat of water utilities being compromised and requires a proactive risk assessment and communication strategy.
