## From the Trenches

First up is the patching of CVE-2026-76461 by Cisco for their Secure Email Gateway, which has already been actively exploited in attacks. This is a stark reminder of the importance of timely patching, especially when it comes to email gateways. With the increasing sophistication of attackers and the rise of ransomware gangs, it's crucial that organizations prioritize patching these vulnerabilities ASAP.

The other story that piqued my interest is the vulnerability in Fortinet's system exploited by a Thai broadband provider. This highlights the need for organizations to regularly review their security posture and ensure that all vendors and systems are up-to-date with the latest patches. It's also a reminder that even seemingly secure solutions can be breached if not properly maintained.

In both cases, it's clear that patching and vulnerability management are critical components of any effective cybersecurity strategy. Organizations must prioritize these efforts to prevent costly breaches and minimize downtime.
