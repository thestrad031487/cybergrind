## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've been following the recent developments in the cat-and-mouse game between hackers and companies that are strengthening their defenses. The fact that AI is sharpening this battle is both fascinating and unsettling. According to reports from BusinessLine, The Times of India, and CNA, companies are now using AI-powered tools to detect and respond to attacks more effectively.

However, the same news outlets also highlight the growing threat of AI-generated exploits, such as the OpenAI AI Pipeline that generated 948 Linux root escalation exploits in just 30 hours. This is a stark reminder that the use of AI for malicious purposes can lead to devastating consequences. As a practitioner, I've seen firsthand how quickly these types of exploits can spread and cause chaos.

The recent warnings from Central cyber security agency about AI-powered frauds targeting banks and fintech firms are also concerning. It's clear that hackers are becoming increasingly sophisticated in their tactics, and companies need to stay ahead of the curve. In this context, the fact that Zyxel has fixed a zero-day exploit that was used to hack nearly 1,000 network switches is a significant development.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** The CISA-identified WordPress RCE flaw is now actively being exploited in attacks, making it essential for organizations to prioritize patching this vulnerability as soon as possible.
