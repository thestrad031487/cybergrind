Zscaler's discovery of a critical NetScaler RCE vulnerability highlights the ongoing threat of third-party supply chain risks to an organization's security posture. The exploitation of this vulnerability, which could allow attackers to execute arbitrary code, underscores the need for robust monitoring and incident response capabilities to detect and respond to similar threats in real-time. A CISO should prioritize ensuring that vendor risk assessments are comprehensive and regularly updated to account for emerging vulnerabilities.

The U.S. CISA's addition of an Atlassian flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog serves as a reminder of the importance of staying up-to-date with the latest security patches and vulnerability advisories. Organizations must ensure that their patch management processes are robust and effective, not only to protect against known vulnerabilities but also to prevent exploitation of zero-day attacks.

A CISO should review their organization's incident response plan to ensure it is comprehensive enough to handle a wide range of cyber threats, including those related to vendor exploits. This includes establishing clear communication channels with affected vendors and stakeholders.

BeyondTrust's patching of a zero-day exploited in attacks since August demonstrates the need for continuous monitoring and security awareness training for employees. A CISO should consider conducting regular security audits and risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities that could be exploited by attackers.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize ongoing cybersecurity risk assessments and incident response planning to stay ahead of emerging threats and protect their business interests.
