The increasing use of artificial intelligence (AI) in cybersecurity is a game-changer for organizations. Visa's expansion of support for its clients and the industry as they navigate this new AI era raises several board-level concerns.

Visa's AI-powered vulnerability patching solution could significantly reduce the risk of data breaches, but it also highlights the need for robust governance around the use of AI in cybersecurity. The lack of standardization and regulation around AI-powered security tools creates a Wild West scenario where organizations may be left vulnerable to attacks if their vendors are not adequately secured.

Furthermore, the reliance on AI-powered vulnerability patching raises questions about vendor risk management. CISOs must ensure that they have a clear understanding of the risks associated with any third-party solutions and have robust due diligence processes in place to mitigate those risks.

Additionally, Visa's announcement also underscores the importance of board-level reporting on cybersecurity risks. As AI-powered security tools become more prevalent, boards will need to be informed about the risks and benefits of these technologies and ensure that they are aligned with the organization's overall risk management strategy.

The expansion of AI-powered vulnerability patching by Visa also raises concerns about insurance coverage for such incidents. CISOs should engage with their insurers to understand how these new tools fit into existing policies and what additional steps need to be taken to ensure adequate coverage.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations must prioritize board-level reporting on the use of AI-powered security tools to ensure transparency and alignment with overall risk management strategy.
