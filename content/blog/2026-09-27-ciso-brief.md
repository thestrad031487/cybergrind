This attack highlights the need for organizations to prioritize vulnerability management and ensure that their systems are up-to-date with the latest security patches. A CISO should conduct a thorough risk assessment and implement a robust patching process to minimize the impact of such vulnerabilities.

In addition to internal patching efforts, CISOs should also consider vendor risk as part of their overall risk management strategy. Regularly reviewing vendor contracts and ensuring that third-party vendors are following best practices for security can help mitigate the risk of similar attacks.

Furthermore, CISOs should ensure that they have adequate insurance coverage in place to protect against such cyberattacks. This includes having a comprehensive insurance policy that covers data breaches, system downtime, and other related losses.

The focus on patching vulnerabilities highlights the importance of maintaining robust security controls across all systems and applications.

Boardroom Takeaway: Organizations should prioritize vulnerability management and ensure they have adequate insurance coverage in place to mitigate the risk of similar attacks.
