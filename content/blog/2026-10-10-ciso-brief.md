The increasing sophistication of cyberattacks is making it imperative for organizations to reassess their vulnerability management processes. The FBI's disruption of Flax Typhoon tools used in critical infrastructure intrusions highlights the evolving threat landscape. As a result, CISOs should prioritize ensuring that their incident response plans are comprehensive enough to address the most advanced threats.

Anthropic's Cyber Mission to defend power grids and open-source code underscores the growing importance of protecting critical infrastructure from cyberattacks. This initiative also emphasizes the need for robust cybersecurity standards in supply chain management, which is particularly relevant given the increasing use of third-party services.

The Hilltop National Bank's closure of Wyoming branches following a cybersecurity incident serves as a reminder of the potential financial and reputational consequences of data breaches. CISOs should consider conducting regular risk assessments to identify vulnerabilities that could impact their organization's ability to operate effectively.

As regulatory bodies continue to strengthen data protection laws, organizations must ensure they are in compliance with these requirements. This includes implementing robust security controls, such as multi-factor authentication and encryption, to safeguard sensitive data.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organisations should prioritize the implementation of multi-factor authentication and encryption measures across their network to mitigate the risk of cyber-attacks on critical infrastructure.
