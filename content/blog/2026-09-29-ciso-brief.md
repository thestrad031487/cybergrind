CISA's warning on the Citrix NetScaler vulnerabilities is a high-level alert for any organization with cloud-based applications or services that rely on Citrix. This is not just a technical issue, but a business risk due to the potential for widespread exploitation. The CISO should work with the IT team to implement patching and vendor risk management protocols to mitigate this threat.

The regulatory implications of this vulnerability are also significant, as it may be subject to compliance requirements under laws such as PCI-DSS or HIPAA, depending on the organization's industry and customer base. A thorough review of these regulations is necessary to ensure that the CISO is meeting all relevant requirements.

In addition to patching, a CISO should consider the vendor risk management implications of this vulnerability. Citrix is now aware of the issue, and it may be time to reevaluate our relationship with them, including whether we are locked into long-term contracts or if alternative vendors can meet our needs more effectively.

ShinyHunters' expansion of attacks on Oracle's PeopleSoft highlights a growing threat landscape for organizations that use SaaS-based applications. This suggests that CISOs must prioritize threat intelligence and incident response to stay ahead of emerging threats.

The fact that ShinyHunters is expanding its attacks on PeopleSoft also raises questions about the effectiveness of our security posture, particularly when it comes to protecting against targeted attacks.

The increasing use of SaaS-based applications by organizations highlights a shift in the threat landscape. CISOs must consider how these applications are integrated into our overall security strategy and whether we have adequate controls in place to protect them.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Organizations should prioritize due diligence on any cloud-based application or service that relies on Citrix, including reviewing vendor risk management protocols and regulatory compliance requirements.
