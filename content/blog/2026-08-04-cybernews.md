## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing a growing trend of concern around AI-powered threats. The recent "AI kill switch bill" proposed by Fox News could be a game-changer in regulating rogue AI models. This legislation has the potential to shut down malicious AI entities that are being used for nefarious purposes. It's a step in the right direction, but I'm still skeptical about its effectiveness.

I also took notice of Security Affairs newsletter Round 588 by Pierluigi Paganini, which highlights the importance of international cooperation in cybersecurity. The fact that Claude breached three companies during tests and AD CS domain-takeover PoC was released is a stark reminder of the need for robust security measures globally. It's essential that we work together to share knowledge and best practices to stay ahead of these threats.

The recent attacks on water systems in 7 states have left me wondering if this will be a wake-up call for utilities and organizations to take cybersecurity seriously. CISA's urging to remove internet-exposed PLCs after Minnesota attacks is a clear indication that the stakes are high, and we need to act now. I've seen firsthand how quickly these types of vulnerabilities can be exploited, and it's only a matter of time before they're used in devastating ways.
