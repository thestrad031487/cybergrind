## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I've seen firsthand how quickly vulnerabilities can be exploited by attackers. Recently, I came across an article that made me think twice about my own security posture - "You're easier to find than you think" (Americanthinker.com). The idea that I'm more vulnerable than I realize is a sobering one, and it's a reminder that no system is completely secure.

Another story caught my eye because of its potential impact on operational technology. Nozomi partnering with Sophos to put OT data in IT consoles (SiliconANGLE News) could be a game-changer for organizations that struggle to keep their ICS systems up-to-date. By integrating OT data into the same consoles used for IT, these companies may finally have a way to get ahead of potential threats before they turn into disasters.

It's not just about patching vulnerabilities, though - it's also about having a robust vulnerability management program in place. The recent change by CISA (Nextgov) is a good reminder that we need to be prepared for the latest developments in the world of cybersecurity. And speaking of preparedness, I was interested to see GAO official suggest addressing AI risks through existing legislation (Nextgov). This could be an important step towards mitigating some of the most pressing threats facing us today.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** Microsoft patches LegacyHive Windows zero-day vulnerability - this one matters because it's a critical fix for a widely-used system, and I'd recommend prioritizing patching for any organization that uses Windows.
