The rising threat of nation-state sponsored cyberattacks poses a significant risk to the US government and its agencies. China's growing cyber capabilities, as highlighted in "How China and America Can Move from Competition to Stability," underscore the need for a robust defense strategy. A breach like CyberXero's, which stole over 628,000 Ukrainian records using Claude code, demonstrates the potential consequences of such attacks.

A CISO should prioritize vendor risk assessments to ensure that third-party partners can be trusted with sensitive data. This includes conducting thorough background checks on vendors and monitoring their security postures for any signs of compromise. Furthermore, regular testing and validation of these relationships is essential to prevent similar breaches in the future.

The increasing importance of data security has led to a growing demand for tighter controls among clients, as seen in "EY data breach puts spotlight on data security at Big Four." Companies like EY must demonstrate their ability to protect sensitive information and maintain the trust of their clients.

As the threat landscape continues to evolve, it is essential for organizations to stay vigilant and invest in robust cybersecurity measures. This includes implementing advanced threat detection systems, such as those offered by Fortinet, which leverage MITRE ATT&CK TTPs for Threat-Informed SecOps. By doing so, companies can improve their ability to detect and respond to emerging threats.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The board should prioritize regular audits of third-party vendors to ensure they are meeting necessary security standards.
