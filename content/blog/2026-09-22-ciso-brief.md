The increasing threat of drone warfare and its implications for national security and cybersecurity are a major concern. As drones become more sophisticated and capable, the risk of them being used for malicious purposes increases, potentially leading to a major disruption in global security.

The potential impact on businesses is significant, as drones could be used to disrupt supply chains, cause physical harm, or even be used as a means of espionage. Companies must consider how they can mitigate these risks and ensure that their operations are not vulnerable to drone-based threats.

A CISO should work closely with the board to develop policies and procedures for handling drone-related security incidents, including incident response plans and procedures for dealing with compromised systems. The CISO should also engage with vendors to ensure that any systems or software used by the company are secure against drone-related threats.

Regulatory bodies such as the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) will likely play a key role in establishing guidelines and regulations for drone use, which could have significant implications for businesses operating in the US.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The board should consider allocating resources to develop policies and procedures for handling drone-related security incidents.
