Visa's expansion of support for its clients and the industry navigating the new AI era of cybersecurity is a significant development that warrants close attention from board-level leaders.

The implications of this move are far-reaching, particularly in terms of regulatory compliance. As Visa integrates more AI-powered security solutions into its ecosystem, it will be essential to ensure that these tools comply with relevant regulations such as GDPR and CCPA. This may require revisiting existing policies and procedures to ensure alignment with evolving standards.

From a business risk perspective, the adoption of AI-driven cybersecurity measures by major players like Visa carries significant implications for organizations relying on these services. Companies must assess the potential risks and benefits of partnering with entities that are leveraging AI in their security offerings, and consider strategies for mitigating any associated threats.

CISOs should prioritize ensuring that their organization's data governance practices remain robust and resilient in the face of emerging technologies like AI.

A CISO should engage in a dialogue with Visa to determine the scope and timeline of support, establish clear contractual agreements outlining compliance requirements, and review existing vendor risk management strategies to ensure adequate coverage for AI-powered solutions.

CISOs should also monitor developments in this space closely and consider engaging industry peers or relevant regulatory bodies to discuss potential implications and recommendations.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** The board should prioritize assessing the regulatory compliance implications of AI-driven cybersecurity solutions used by key vendors like Visa.
