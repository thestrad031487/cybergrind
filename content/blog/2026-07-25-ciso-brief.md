CISA adds Microsoft SharePoint and Check Point SmartConsole flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. This development highlights the importance of prioritizing vulnerability management in our organization, particularly for products like Microsoft SharePoint that are widely used across various industries.

The addition of these vulnerabilities to CISA's catalog also underscores the need for a proactive approach to security testing and patching. A thorough risk assessment should be conducted to identify potential vulnerabilities and develop a mitigation strategy, including implementing patches, updating software configurations, and enhancing network defenses.

A CISO should also engage with vendors like Microsoft and Check Point to understand their remediation plans and provide input on the impact of these vulnerabilities on our organization's systems and data. Regular board reporting and budget updates are essential to ensure that security investments align with business objectives and regulatory requirements.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Regularly reviewing and updating incident response plans to account for emerging threats like zero-click attacks is crucial to minimize the risk of significant financial losses due to data breaches.
