CISO Briefing - November 2026

Cisco BroadWorks and Openwave Flaws Actively Exploited Despite Available Patches
The recent exploits of vulnerabilities in Cisco BroadWorks and Openwave systems highlight the need for robust vulnerability management practices. Boards should be concerned about the potential financial losses resulting from failed incident response, as well as the risk of reputational damage to their organization. CISOs should prioritize updating these systems with available patches and developing a comprehensive incident response plan.

EU cyber scores look healthy, but 45% of surveyed IT providers take months to patch
The EU's cyber security posture appears robust, but there is still room for improvement in terms of proactive vulnerability management. Boards should investigate the root causes of this delay and consider implementing more stringent policies around patching timelines. CISOs can work with vendors to develop more effective patch management processes.

U.S. CISA adds IBM, SignalWire FreeSwitch, OpenSSH, and GPUStack flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog
The addition of these newly exploited vulnerabilities to the U.S. CISA's catalog serves as a reminder that even highly regarded organizations are not immune to cyber threats. Boards should review their vendors' security posture and ensure that they have adequate incident response plans in place. CISOs can use this list to prioritize patching efforts and develop more comprehensive security assessments.

**Boardroom Takeaway:** Regularly reviewing vendor security posture is crucial to maintaining the overall organization's resilience against emerging threats.
