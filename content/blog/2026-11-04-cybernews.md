## From the Trenches

As a cybersecurity practitioner, I'm seeing two trends that are making my job more interesting - and more challenging. First, hackers are targeting something we all rely on: food. The fact that supermarkets in Europe have been hijacked by hackers is a clear indication that no organization is immune to cyber threats. This is not just about the financial impact; it's also about the potential for physical harm if these systems are compromised.

The scale of this attack is staggering, and it highlights the need for organizations to take their cybersecurity posture seriously. In particular, supermarkets need to consider how they're protecting their refrigeration systems from cyber threats. The implications of a successful attack could be disastrous, not just for the business but also for public health.

This incident is a wake-up call for all organizations that rely on critical infrastructure. It's a reminder that cybersecurity is no longer just about preventing financial losses; it's about ensuring the safety and well-being of people. If you're in charge of protecting your organization's systems, take this threat seriously and prioritize your patching efforts.

**🔧 Patch Priority:** The supermarket freezers affected by the hack are vulnerable to a known vulnerability in the industrial control system (ICS) used in their refrigeration systems.
